Our platform funds an app wallet, moves money into recipients' wallets, and pays recipients out. Payout fees
are charged to the recipient: ACH costs $0.25; push-to-card costs $1.00 + 0.75%, at least $1.50. A recipient
flagged by compliance can't be paid out. No wallet may go negative, fees included, even when payouts race. A
failed payout returns the money and its fee to the recipient's wallet, once. In a batch, one refused payout
must not affect the others. We don't have accounts with any new services yet. Build it so it runs here, and
show it working, including a refused payout.
