Viewers subscribe to creators' membership tiers, monthly. On every charge we keep a 5% platform fee and pay
2.9% + $0.30 processing; the creator earns the rest, available after a 7-day hold. A payout to a creator costs
$1. Each subscription period is charged once, even if the renewal job runs twice. A charge's refunds plus any
chargeback can never exceed the charge; a chargeback the creator can't cover becomes debt, and new earnings pay
the debt before any payout. Our own example: 20 members at $50 leave the creator $915.00, and one payout
delivers $914.00. We don't have accounts with any new services yet. Build it so it runs here, and show it
working, including our example and a payout refused while the creator is in debt.
